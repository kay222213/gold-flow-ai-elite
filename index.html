RISK & DAILY LIMITS

CONFIGURABLE

Risk % sets the cash-risk budget and position size when verified contract specs are available. It does not move entry, stop-loss or take-profit prices. Those levels are derived from structure, ATR, minimum stop distance and reward:risk validation.

Daily P/L is manual input, not read from a broker. Without the private backend settings token, changes remain local and do not change server-side signal gates. Settings do not place orders or enforce broker-side limits.
